## Newly reusable material and J86 boundaries

- **Pinned source/build structure:** Orchard base `a6ffc904bf8acb7af2fd0fc94f84ca194bd82ed2`
  and Linux base `4cec5aa77c69ac1fd7c605ab8159714ea9024a08`, with integration
  snapshots and a gauge-cache wait delta. The tested kernel is
  `7.3.0-rc1-j72-pw1-orchard-apple-4k`. Preserve source receipts and 4 KiB ABI;
  never mix these modules with our 7.0.12 J86 kernels. A complete clean-checkout
  installed deployment is not yet qualified even for J72.
- **Audio:** `code/current/audio/` publishes MCA-v1, an A7 SmartIO variant,
  DMA dependencies, DT overlay, build recipe and guarded runtime staging.
  J72 reuses CS42L81; six-command setup and two DMA channels are reported.
  These are valuable references for native integration and lifecycle review,
  not a replacement for our measured J86 eight-command transport and private
  speaker/firmware records. Both J72 channel tones were heard and PipeWire
  playback passed; exact physical separation was hard to judge. Fresh-boot
  service startup, browser audio, headphones and microphone remain unqualified.
  Hot-overlay teardown has an unresolved reference warning; do not repeatedly
  unload/reload it. Our existing J86 speaker/volume evidence is unchanged.
- **Charging:** `code/current/charging/` supplies source admission, supervisor,
  per-installation `PENDING` profiles, synthetic tests and external-power
  reporting. Reuse its review/test approach, not its hardware pins or current
  limits. Its nominal 1,050 mA input policy is specific to the qualified powered
  JCH377 port; this is not battery current and not a setting for Perry's Mac.
  A later browser workload delayed a status read, tripped the watchdog and
  stopped charging with baseline restoration. No unattended Linux charging
  claim is justified. Do not bypass timing/thermal protections to port it.
- **Battery:** HDQ/UART gauge and cache-wait source can inform a future J86
  implementation, after exact gauge/PMIC/UART and unit/current-sign review.
  J72 reports percentage, voltage, signed current, temperature and external
  power. Stale supervisor snapshots are not live battery evidence or proof
  of charging. J86 reporting remains unqualified.
- **Wi-Fi and reconnect:** `code/current/wifi/` supplies hardware ordering
  before unchanged NetworkManager, pinned private firmware metadata and
  lifecycle checks. Compare it with our J86 HSIC/BCM43241 work; do not substitute
  J72 parameters or firmware metadata. Charging reconnect admission is a useful
  source-loss/reattach reference. J86 USB reconnect still requires its own
  bounded tests, particularly with a Mac-backed root and pending app writes.
- **Storage:** current snapshots now include experimental ASP write policy,
  bounds, recovery fencing and installed-root integration. This supersedes the
  November 6 observation that only the older read-only source was published.
  The snapshots are not a complete reviewed UUID-link fix for J86, and no such
  fix was applied here. Retain the offline root-identity audit and separately
  review early-boot UUID/PARTUUID discovery and exact-board ASP behavior.

J72 is `iPad4,2`, CPID 0x8960, BDID 0x12; our J86 is `iPad4,5`, BDID 0x0c.
J85/J86m are additional targets, not aliases. The same A7 family does not make
DT resources, calibration, PMIC settings or storage layouts interchangeable.
No J72 patch, service or auto-enable setting is imported by this checkpoint.

## Consequences for Perry

The next steps are unchanged: inspect USB, use DFU plus the known-good RAM
**desktop** when needed, then HDMI, Bluetooth/wired inputs, orientation,
auto-rotation and suspend. Prepare a host-independent RAM environment before
an HDMI cable swap; the new J72 reconnect report does not make disconnecting
our Mac-backed root safe. See [the ordered queue](../HANDS-ON-QUEUE.md).

Internal storage stays a separate future decision. **Verified backup and Perry's
explicit yes are required** before snapshot deletion, APFS resize, GPT edits,
write tests, formatting or installation. APFS free space is not a safe shrink
limit. Cam's retained iOS fallback and 50 GB result do not guarantee another
iPad's recovery. No internal-write procedure is authorized or scheduled here.
