# Orchard and Omarchy A v0.3.0: Mini 2 review

Reviewed 11 November 2026, host-side only. No USB inventory, device connection,
boot, firmware activation or internal-storage operation was performed.

**Keep the known-good RAM-boot profile unchanged.** The releases add useful
porting targets, not a qualified replacement for our J86 payload. No kernel,
DTB, loader, firmware, desktop package, charging setting or power policy changes
are included in this checkpoint.

## J85 report: useful evidence, not an Air or J86 recipe

Orchard's release notes call J85 an iPad Air. The linked
[J85 report](https://github.com/OrchardLinux/orchard/blob/71502a02fdfd3ac6e2cce3ea6c16d6211398f02e/docs/bring-up/j85.md)
actually identifies Mini 2 Wi-Fi / iPad4,4 / A1489. It records tethered RAM boots,
a simpledrm CPU desktop, aligned touch, buttons and Wi-Fi SSH on local Hoolock
7.3 test builds through 27 October. Its bounded 1 A wall-adapter trials are not
a current-limit recommendation, sustained charging qualification, or proof of
safe computer-USB operation. USB/NBD reliability, GPU frames, Bluetooth, audio
and installed boot remain unqualified there.

The report explicitly lacks a clean Orchard producer and redistributable RAM
recipe. Do not import its runtime9 bundle, charger settings or board data.
The separate [J86 report](https://github.com/OrchardLinux/orchard/blob/71502a02fdfd3ac6e2cce3ea6c16d6211398f02e/docs/bring-up/j86.md)
already records our Pomme/Hoolock 7.0.12, 4 KiB, software-rendered desktop and
later Wi-Fi/isolated speaker results. It explicitly does not validate Orchard's
kernel, recovery, converter, CLI or installer. Neither report is a new hardware
test from this review.

## Boot changes: reusable host entry versus installed-system boot manager

[Boot entry selection](https://github.com/OrchardLinux/orchard/blob/71502a02fdfd3ac6e2cce3ea6c16d6211398f02e/docs/platforms-and-builds.md#boot-methods-and-entries)
now accepts `--via checkra1n` (or `ORCHARD_VIA`) for run/install/launch/boot.
This selects the route into Pongo, not an installer permission or a new Linux
payload. J86's preference is checkra1n; the generic fallback is palera1n.
The [implementation](https://github.com/OrchardLinux/orchard/blob/71502a02fdfd3ac6e2cce3ea6c16d6211398f02e/src/orchard/apple/entries.py)
uses standalone checkra1n with `-E -p -v -n -k`; its comment identifies J72 as
the only measured use of that entry so far. Tools are fetched and SHA-256 pinned
per host; explicit binary overrides bypass normal selection intentionally.
There is no automatic retry through another entry after failure. This is a
future exact-J86 RAM experiment, not a reason to replace our qualified loader.

Separately, m1n1 now reads Boot Loader Specification entries from internal
XBOOTLDR storage, paints the splash, loads the selected kernel/initrd/DTB and
starts an installed root. The host selects the entry. The
[boot contract](https://github.com/OrchardLinux/orchard/blob/71502a02fdfd3ac6e2cce3ea6c16d6211398f02e/docs/boot-contract.md)
was hardware-tested on J71b, not J86. `orchard launch` is an installed-system
path, not our Mac NBD RAM desktop. Launch itself is documented read-only;
creating its boot/root partitions and entries requires installer writes.
No such layout is prepared here. The NVMe boot-from-host strap fix and d221
ANS2 storage work do not establish A7 ASP storage support.

**Any internal-storage write requires Perry's explicit yes**, a verified backup,
exact-board storage qualification and a reviewed recovery/write plan. This
includes creating boot entries, snapshot deletion, APFS resize, GPT edits,
formatting and installation. Do not run launch/install as a shortcut in this
RAM-only workflow; even a read-only storage experiment needs its own review.

## Generic suspend: source support, not proven J86 sleep

The pinned [suspend bring-up guide](https://github.com/OrchardLinux/orchard/blob/71502a02fdfd3ac6e2cce3ea6c16d6211398f02e/docs/bring-up/suspend.md)
describes s2idle, not a firmware deep-sleep state. Orchard extends GPIO/display
power-domain retention and Apple cpuidle coverage to S5L8960X and other SoCs.
Only T8010 is tested in that guide. Every board may attempt suspend regardless
of its feature-state label; untested boards may reboot or never wake. Keeping
domains powered is not evidence of measured power savings.

Our qualified kernel lacks CONFIG_SUSPEND. The separate suspend1 candidate is
built but unbooted; do not enable it, copy A10 domains, or add idle suspend to
the desktop now. Port the A7-specific source into a separate candidate only
after reviewing GPIO/display ownership, wake IRQs, cpuidle and every periodic
poller/shared SPI/UART/SIO owner. The A10 gauge-poll fix is a useful audit pattern,
not proof our private modules suspend safely.

After HDMI, Bluetooth and rotation work, use only a self-contained RAM root:
flush/detach the Mac app disk and NBD before testing. Check devices/platform
pm_test stages before real sleep. The guide's acceptance evidence includes:
same boot ID, expected button wake IRQ, unchanged failure count, visible display
and touch recovery, increased s2idle usage, and CLOCK_BOOTTIME minus
CLOCK_MONOTONIC increasing by the sleep duration. Wait at least 45 seconds
before wake and include a sleep longer than three minutes to catch watchdog
failures; verify USB, Wi-Fi and other enabled peripherals after resume.
Screen blanking or a reconnect alone does not pass. No test ran in this review.

## Omarchy desktop changes and next work

GPU rendering and Firefox hardware video are advertised for iPad 6/7, not A7.
J71b AOP/IIO auto-rotation is not an Oscar/M7 UART4 driver. J81 Bluetooth and
J96/J97 peripherals are not J86 qualifications. Broadcom PCIe regulatory fixes
do not qualify our USB BCM4324 HSIC transport.

The app switcher, touch Files, keyboard, rotation lock, lock/idle behavior and
package-download retries are possible later userspace ports. They require a
separate image and compatibility tests with our Labwc/touch profile. Omarchy's
[new power daemon](https://github.com/OrchardLinux/omarchy-a/blob/84aa9766bda4b7806ec3ef65c979f654ae880b05/system/overlay/usr/libexec/omarchy-powerd)
asks logind CanSuspend before requesting suspend, but that is a capability check,
not exact-board wake qualification. Do not import its sleep-after-screen-off
policy into our tethered desktop or update the verified read-only root in place.

Next physical work remains: fresh DFU + known-good RAM desktop check, then HDMI,
then Bluetooth peripherals, then four orientations, auto-rotation and finally
isolated suspend. Before HDMI cable swaps, prepare a self-contained RAM session;
never pull the cable from an active Mac-backed root. No new release default is
required to resume that queue.
